Song and video workflows in Umicom Music

The creative workspace contains two shared Framework pages:

  • Song and video plan: enter timed lyrics and a manual tempo, inspect shot boundaries, save/reopen a plan and export SRT captions.
  • PixVerse videos: use a locally saved API key to review a text-to-video request and explicitly check its job status.

Follow the song planning guide and PixVerse guide in the Applications checkout for the numbered workflows and recovery steps.

Save private plans outside the source repository. Song plans contain no audio; PixVerse results are not automatically downloaded, assembled or imported. These pages do not connect to Suno or SONDO. Local writing and timing need no provider account. PixVerse requires API access and credits.

The portable owners are available to other creative applications. Music composes the shared native pages; it does not keep a duplicate provider client or credential database.
